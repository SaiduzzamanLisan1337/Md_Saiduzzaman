About

I started in IT security operations — access control, device hardening, and SIEM monitoring — before moving into vulnerability assessment and digital forensics, investigating fraud, identity theft, and data breach cases using tools like Autopsy, Cellebrite, and FTK. That hands-on investigative work is what pulled me toward research: my current M.Sc. thesis looks at making self-supervised intrusion detection systems robust against unreliable or adversarial feedback in the human-in-the-loop, and I collaborate on applied papers spanning intrusion detection, malware detection with LLMs and vision-language models, and secure systems for wireless and cloud environments.
