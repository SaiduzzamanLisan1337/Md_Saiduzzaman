01 / PROFILE

Academic Profile

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Information Technology, with a major in Artificial Intelligence, at The Kyoto College of Graduate Studies for Informatics (KCGI) in Kyoto, Japan. My academic interests focus on applying machine learning and intelligent systems to cybersecurity, particularly anomaly-based network intrusion detection.

My academic background combines artificial intelligence, information systems security, and computer science. This is complemented by almost four years of professional experience in cybersecurity and IT security, including vulnerability assessment, digital forensics, incident investigation, security monitoring, access control, system hardening, and security reporting.

I am interested in research that connects rigorous machine-learning methods with practical security challenges and produces reliable, robust, and useful security systems.
