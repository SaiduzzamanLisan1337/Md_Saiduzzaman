01 / ABOUT

Academic Profile

I am an M.Sc. student in Information Technology, majoring in Artificial Intelligence, at The Kyoto College of Graduate Studies for Informatics in Kyoto, Japan. My academic interests are focused on applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to cybersecurity problems, particularly the analysis and detection of anomalous network activity.

My academic direction is complemented by nearly four years of professional experience in cybersecurity and IT security. This experience has included vulnerability assessment, security monitoring, incident investigation, log and network-traffic analysis, access control, system hardening, and digital forensics.

I am interested in research that connects rigorous machine-learning methods with practical security challenges and produces reliable, explainable, and useful security systems.
