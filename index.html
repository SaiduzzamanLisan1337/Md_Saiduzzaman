Master’s Thesis Research — KCGI

Byzantine-Robust Human-in-the-Loop Calibration for Continual Self-Supervised Intrusion Detection

Current focus: robust and continual learning for intrusion detection, combining self-supervised learning with human-in-the-loop calibration and Byzantine robustness.

  • Investigating continual and self-supervised learning approaches for adaptive network intrusion detection.
  • Developing a human-in-the-loop calibration mechanism to incorporate analyst feedback into model adaptation.
  • Studying robustness against Byzantine or unreliable feedback in collaborative learning environments.
