01 / Research direction

Questions worth
working on.

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and cybersecurity, with a particular interest in systems that remain useful when data, environments, or human input cannot be fully trusted.

Current research focus

Byzantine-robust human-in-the-loop calibration for continual self-supervised intrusion detection.

How can an intrusion detection system keep learning from analyst feedback while resisting unreliable or strategically manipulated labels, adapting to concept drift, and retaining useful historical knowledge?
